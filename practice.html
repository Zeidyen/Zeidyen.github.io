Five things to try on your phone. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere: it all runs on your own device. Each one goes with a week of the lecture notes, so try the demo first and then read the week it belongs to.

Statistical Thinking · Week 5 · relationships

Guess the correlation

Look at the scatter and estimate r, the correlation. Drag the slider, then press Check. Ten rounds. Most people are poor at this to begin with and much better after twenty minutes, which is the point.

Statistical Thinking · Week 3 and Week 9 · sampling

Why bigger samples settle down

The top plot is a population: lopsided, like most real measurements. Draw samples from it and the bottom plot collects their means. Watch what happens to the spread of that second plot as you raise the sample size.

Press a button to start drawing samples.

Statistical Thinking · Week 3 · sampling design

Sample it yourself

Below is a field of 240 boreholes. Each has a yield you cannot see, and yields are higher in the north. Your job is to estimate the average yield from a sample of 20. Try each method several times and watch where its estimates land.
