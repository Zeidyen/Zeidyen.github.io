Ghana SMC Policy Dashboard

Modelled impact, burden averted and value-for-money of adding a fifth seasonal malaria chemoprevention cycle and extending eligibility beyond under-fives. Pick a region on the map, then move coverage, delivery cost and willingness-to-pay to see the numbers shift.

Ghana Mosquito Insecticide Resistance Dashboard

Insecticide resistance in Ghanaian mosquito populations, filtered by environmental location and insecticide type. Reports total samples, overall resistance and dominant species, with tabs for a resistance map, species and genetics, and insecticide efficacy.
